# Social Media Strategy — ResGro-AI

## 1. Product Name & Category
**Product:** ResGro-AI
**Category:** AI-powered Resume Growth & Optimization (Career/SaaS)

## 2. Target Audience
**Who are we selling to?**
- Job seekers and professionals looking to optimize their resumes.
- Students and recent graduates.
- Career switchers needing a competitive edge.

## 3. Primary Distribution Channels
- **LinkedIn:** The primary platform for professional networking and resume sharing.
- **Twitter/X:** Engaging with the tech job community and sharing resume tips.
- **Reddit:** Focused subreddits like r/resumes and r/careeradvice.
- **TikTok/Instagram Reels:** Sharing quick resume hacks and "Before/After" transformations.

## 4. Content Strategy
- **"The Resume Glow-Up":** Before and after transformations showing the impact of AI optimization.
- **ATS-Friendly Formatting:** Educational content on how to make your resume readable by hiring bots.
- **Keyword Optimization Guides:** How to tailor your resume for specific job descriptions.
- **User Success Stories:** Highlighting users who landed interviews after using ResGro-AI.

## 5. Growth Hack / Viral Loop
**The "Free Audit" Loop:**
Offer a free "Resume Score" or "Audit" to users who share a link to ResGro-AI on their LinkedIn profile. This generates social proof and drives high-intent traffic from the user's professional network.
